# Data Analysis with Python

## Assignment Scenario

In this assignment, you will assume the role of a **Data Analyst** working for a **Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT)** that is planning to invest in residential properties. Your objective is to analyze housing data and develop predictive models to estimate the market price of a house based on various features such as square footage, number of bedrooms, number of floors, and other property attributes. A template Jupyter Notebook is provided, and your task is to complete the assigned analytical exercises.

---

## Dataset Used in this Assignment

The dataset contains house sale prices from **King County, USA**, including the city of Seattle. It covers homes sold between **May 2014 and May 2015**. The dataset was sourced from **Kaggle** and slightly modified for the purposes of this course.

### Dataset:
- **House Sales in King County, USA**
